GLORIOUS MEN'S RETREAT 2026 - PHONE APP (Android and iPhone)

This folder is the app. Put it on the internet once and everyone opens it from one link.

1. Upload this whole folder to a free web host that gives you an https link. The easiest:
   Netlify - sign up free at netlify.com, then drag this folder onto the Deploy area. (Create the free account so the site is kept.)
   Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages also work.
2. Open the link on your phone and test it.
3. Share the link in the WhatsApp group.

Participants:
- Android (Chrome): tap "Install the app" at the top of the app.
- iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
After that it opens from the home screen with the icon, in one tap, and keeps working without signal.

Connecting the data collection:
1. Follow "SETUP - read me first.txt" in the backend folder to get your Web app URL.
2. Open config.js here, paste the URL after BACKEND_URL, save, and upload the folder again.

Changing the program: edit the Program tab in the Google Sheet. The app updates by itself.
After uploading a changed folder, change gmr26-v2 to gmr26-v3 in sw.js so phones fetch the new files.
